# A 60-second pitch

**0–10 seconds:** “Welcome to Fly Swarm Kitchen. Eight tiny virtual flies have been given a ridiculous job: carry a pea. Each fly pushes at its own point on this beam. Gravity and torque decide what happens.” Start Lift & Balance with Autopilot.

**10–20 seconds:** Apply a gust and remove one fly. “This is our engineering reference controller. The beam recovers through forces, not an animation path.”

**20–35 seconds:** Select Carry a Pea and Learned action readout. Restart the scenario. “This controller sends engineered observations through 1,362 real fly-connectome connections. We trained separate force readouts for all eight flies by imitating the reference controller. The anatomical connections themselves stay fixed.”

**35–45 seconds:** Select a fly and point to its activity/readout display. Reset learned memory, then restart. “Erase those learned output weights and the policy disappears. A connectome alone does not come with a tiny chef program.”

**45–60 seconds:** Click Train readout, then run the judge. “Our frozen benchmark succeeded in 17 of 20 held-out scenarios. It failed all four harder stress cases; the engineering teacher passed all 24. That gives us a fun demo, a real computational dependency on anatomy, and an honest limit—not evidence that biological wiring is superior.”

The live judge is a new eight-second physical run with a deterministic gust. Its actual outcome may differ from the 12-second benchmark. Do not narrate a success before PASS appears.
